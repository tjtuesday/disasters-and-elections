Before you search

  • This is FEMA data. It holds federal major disaster declarations only (Stafford Act, declaration type DR). It does not include FEMA emergency declarations, fire management declarations or any state-level emergency declaration by a governor.
  • A declaration is included when its incident period touched Sept. 1 through Nov. 30 of an even-numbered year, 1976 to 2024. The 2020 COVID-19 declarations were removed.
  • Senate and presidential margins come from the MIT Election Data and Science Lab, which compiles 50 years of returns and gives them away free. A disaster and an election in the same state and fall says nothing about whether one affected the other.
  • FEMA's incident period is FEMA's call. Treat "active on Election Day" as the outer bound, not a measure of what any office was dealing with that day.
